Workout guide

Complete all five exercises in sequence. Rest only after a full round.

Schedule

  • Train 2–3 times per week.
  • Take at least one full rest day between workouts.
  • Complete 3 rounds per session.

Warm-up

Complete 2 rounds, resting about 30 seconds between rounds:

  • 10 arm swings each direction, per arm
  • 10 torso rotations
  • 8 prayer squats
  • 30-second Spiderman stretch per side

Rest

  • Do not rest between individual exercises unless needed for safety.
  • Rest 60–90 seconds after completing each full round.

Progression

  • Good form with RPE 6–7: keep the same weight or increase slightly.
  • If all three rounds feel easy: add weight or add 1–2 reps, not both.
  • If you miss reps: repeat the same weight next time.
  • If form breaks down: reduce weight, reps, pace, or range of motion.
  • At RPE 8–10: maintain or reduce weight and consider more rest.

Safety

Stop the exercise if you feel sharp pain. Prioritize controlled reps and good form over completing a target.
